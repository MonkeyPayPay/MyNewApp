Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026  ·  Effective: June 30, 2026

CareCircle ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of caregiving families. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

CareCircle, Inc. operates the CareCircle web application and mobile apps (the "Service"), a family coordination platform for elder care. We are based in the United States.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information You Provide

  • Account information — email address, name, and optional profile details when you sign up.
  • Care circle data — the name of the person you care for, care log entries, tasks, appointments, and expenses you add.
  • Documents — files you upload to the Document Vault (PDFs, images, etc.).
  • Notifications preferences — whether you opt into push notifications or email alerts.
  • Billing information — handled directly by Stripe; we never see or store your credit card details.

2b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps).
  • Device information (model, OS version) when using our mobile app.
  • Usage analytics to improve the product (we use privacy-respecting, aggregated methods).

3. Third-Party Services We Use

🗄️ Supabase
Authentication, PostgreSQL database, and file storage. Your care data lives in Supabase's hosted infrastructure. Supabase is SOC 2 Type II certified. Privacy policy
💳 Stripe
Payment processing and subscription management. When you subscribe, you interact with Stripe's checkout. We receive only a customer ID and subscription status — never your full card details. Privacy policy
✉️ Resend
Transactional email delivery (magic-link sign-in emails, care alerts). Resend receives your email address to deliver messages. Privacy policy
🧠 Anthropic / Claude AI
The AI Care Advisor feature sends anonymised summaries of your care log entries (not names or personal identifiers) to Anthropic's Claude API to generate insights. We do not send raw document contents to Claude. Anthropic processes this data under API terms that prohibit training on submitted data. Privacy policy

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the CareCircle Service.
  • To authenticate you and manage your subscription.
  • To send transactional emails (sign-in links, care alerts you opt into).
  • To generate AI Care Advisor insights (Family and Pro plans only).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use care log entries or health-related data for advertising purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 3, as required to operate the Service. Family members you invite to your care circle can see the care data for that circle. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect safety.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data and care circle data are deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Files in the Document Vault are deleted from storage within 7 days of account deletion.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including TLS in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest (via Supabase), row-level security on all database tables, and private (non-public) storage buckets for documents. No system is perfectly secure; we encourage you to use a strong, unique password and protect your email account.

8. Health Information Notice

CareCircle is a family coordination tool, not a covered entity under HIPAA. We handle care notes and documents with care, but if you upload Protected Health Information (PHI), you do so at your own discretion. We recommend using the platform for coordination and logistics rather than as a formal medical records system.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@carecircle.app. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children

CareCircle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you by email or in-app notice before significant changes take effect. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
